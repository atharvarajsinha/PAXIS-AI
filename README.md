# PAXIS AI

### Personalized AI Learning Journey

PAXIS AI helps people turn their learning goals into a **personalized, step-by-step learning journey**.

Instead of being confused about **what to learn, where to learn it, and what to do next**, users can simply tell PAXIS AI what they want to achieve.

---

## 🌟 How PAXIS AI Works

### 1. Create Your Profile

The user starts by creating their personal profile.

They can tell PAXIS about:

* Their interests
* Their current level of knowledge
* What they have already learned
* What they want to achieve
* How much time they can spend learning each week

This helps PAXIS understand **who the learner is and where they are starting from**.

---

### 2. Tell PAXIS Your Goal

The user simply describes what they want to achieve.

They don't need to know any special commands or follow a complicated process.

For example:

> **"Mujhe 6 mahine mein Python Developer banna hai."**

The user can communicate naturally in **English, Hindi, or Hinglish**.

---

### 3. PAXIS Understands the Goal

PAXIS looks at the learner's goal together with their profile.

For example, two people may have the same goal of becoming a Python Developer, but:

* One may be a complete beginner.
* Another may already know programming.
* One may have 2 hours per week.
* Another may have 10 hours per week.

PAXIS creates the journey according to **that particular learner**.

---

### 4. Get a Personalized Learning Journey

PAXIS creates a clear learning journey divided into different stages.

Each stage explains what the learner should focus on and what they should accomplish before moving forward.

The learner can see:

* What to learn
* What to practice
* What to complete
* What comes next
* Important milestones along the way

This turns a large goal into **small, manageable steps**.

---

### 5. Find Learning Resources

PAXIS can provide useful resources for the different stages of the journey.

For example:

* Helpful websites
* Educational videos
* Learning materials

The learner can open these resources directly and start learning.

If suitable resources cannot be found, the learning journey can still be provided without them.

---

### 6. Save the Learning Journey

Once the learner receives a journey they want to follow, they can choose:

> **Track This Roadmap**

The journey is then saved to their personal learning space.

This means they can return to it later without starting again.

---

### 7. Complete Each Step

As the learner studies, they can mark completed stages.

For example:

**Stage 1 — Completed ✓**
**Stage 2 — Completed ✓**
**Stage 3 — In Progress**
**Stage 4 — Not Started**

The learner can therefore clearly see **where they currently are**.

---

### 8. Track Your Progress

PAXIS continuously shows the learner's progress.

The learner can see:

* Overall completion
* Progress in different skills
* Completed milestones
* Recent learning activity
* Learning streak
* What they should do next

This makes learning feel like a journey rather than an endless list of topics.

---

### 9. Get Your Next Action

One of the most important parts of PAXIS is helping the learner answer:

> **"What should I do next?"**

Instead of searching for the next thing to study, PAXIS recommends the next useful action based on the learner's current progress.

---

### 10. Continue Your Journey Anytime

The learner's conversations and learning journeys are saved.

So they can leave today and come back later.

When they return, they can continue from where they stopped.

---

## 🎯 What Problem Does PAXIS Solve?

Learning something new can often be confusing.

People may ask:

* Where should I start?
* What should I learn first?
* What should I learn next?
* Am I learning the right things?
* Which resources should I use?
* How much have I completed?
* What should I do today?

**PAXIS brings these answers together in one personalized learning journey.**
